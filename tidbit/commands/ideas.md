Get ideas for the user's next videos with the tidbit tools.

1. Pick the mix from $ARGUMENTS: `like-ours` is 0, `mix` is 50, `from-others` is 100. With no argument, use 50.
2. Call `my_analytics` first only if the user asks why something worked. Otherwise go straight to `next_video_ideas` with that mix.
3. Show the `line`. Then write each idea in the user's voice: a title, the hook, and three beats. Use the idea's `numbers` as the reason.
4. Ideas from other creators are references. Never reuse a reference's hook or script, and keep everything in its `avoid` list out of your words.
5. Offer `plan_video` with the idea's format id to plan one.
